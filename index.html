Questions we are often asked

Short answers, from our dental chair in Dadabari, Kota. Every case is different, so a consultation comes first.

What is the cost of dental implants in Kota?

The cost depends on how many teeth are being replaced, the condition of your gum and bone, and the type of implant and crown. After an examination and X-ray, Dr. Jaiswal explains the plan and its cost before anything begins.

Is root canal treatment painful?

The treatment is done under local anaesthesia, so the procedure itself is designed to be comfortable. It usually relieves the pain that an infected tooth causes, and it lets you keep your natural tooth.

How long does treatment with braces take?

It varies with the alignment of your teeth and the treatment chosen, and can range from several months to a couple of years. You will get an estimate for your own case at the consultation.

Am I suitable for a dental implant?

Most healthy adults with enough bone and healthy gums can be considered. This can only be confirmed after an examination, which is why we begin with a consultation.

Aftercare guides

General guidance only. Always follow the instructions Dr. Jaiswal gives you for your own case.

After a tooth extraction

Bite gently on the gauze as advised. For the first day, avoid rinsing hard, spitting, using a straw and hot food or drinks. Eat soft food and keep the area clean as instructed.

After root canal treatment

The numbness wears off in a few hours, so avoid chewing until it does. Mild soreness can be normal. Avoid biting hard on the treated tooth until it has its final filling or crown.

With braces

Brush after every meal, and clean around the brackets carefully. Avoid very hard or sticky foods. Keep your adjustment appointments, and call us if a wire or bracket breaks.

After a dental implant

Follow the medicines and cleaning routine you were given. Eat soft food at first, avoid smoking, and attend every review visit so healing can be checked.

Call the clinic the same day if you have swelling that spreads, a fever, bleeding that does not stop, or pain that medicines do not ease. In an emergency, go to the nearest hospital.
